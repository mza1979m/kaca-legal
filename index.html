KACA Privacy Policy

Effective July 16, 2026

KACA is a family chore and allowance tracker. We collect only what's needed to run the app: a parent email address for sign-in, the family and kid names a parent enters, and the chores, allowances, goals, and balances the family tracks. Kids do not have accounts and do not sign in — a kid's PIN is a local mode-switch on the parent's device and is stored hashed, never in plain text.

Optional photos (chore proof, avatars, goal images) are stored privately and are visible only to your own family. If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send them; you can turn this off in your device settings at any time.

We show no ads, sell no data, and share data with no one except the service providers that host the app's database (Supabase) and deliver its emails (Resend), who process it only on our behalf. We collect no data from children beyond the profile a parent creates, and kids are never shown ads or outside links.

Money in KACA is a family scorekeeping tool only — the app moves no real money and connects to no bank. Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your family and all its data at any time by emailing kaca@60apps.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Questions or requests: kaca@60apps.com. This app follows Google Play's Families policy. See also the Terms of Service.
