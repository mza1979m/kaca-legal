KACA helps families track chores and allowance. Balances shown in the app are a record of what a parent has agreed to owe a child — they are not a bank balance and KACA does not hold, move, or guarantee any money. "Cash out" means the parent hands over real cash and marks it paid; settlement happens entirely between family members.

The app is for parents and guardians; you must be an adult to create an account, and you are responsible for the accuracy of the chores, amounts, and payments you record. Use the app only for your own family and keep your sign-in and parent PIN to yourself.

The app is provided as-is, without warranty, and we may update or discontinue features. v1 is free; any future subscription will be clearly disclosed and parent-gated before purchase. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.
